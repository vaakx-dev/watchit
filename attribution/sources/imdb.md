# IMDb image CDN

- Image host: https://m.media-amazon.com/
- Conditions of use: https://www.imdb.com/conditions

Cinemeta search results can supply poster URLs under `/images/M/` on IMDb's image CDN. The app uses the supplied URLs without scraping IMDb or querying its metadata service.

Source URLs and available credit fields are retained. Image availability does not establish a reuse license.
